Little Us privacy notice

Effective October 18, 2026. Little Us is a private Android app for Alex and Pam. Alex Ponce develops and administers the app. Contact aleponce92@gmail.com for privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requests.

What the app stores

Little Us uses Google sign-in and Firebase Authentication to identify the two authorized household members. The service processes Google account identifiers and sign-in information, which can include an email address, name and profile image. Little Us stores the account identifier and household membership needed to authorize access.

The app stores the categories, activities, points, logged acts, competition results, prize credits and prize-use records you create. Historical notes and reward text remain in existing records even though new note inputs have been removed. It also stores personal favorites and app preferences.

If you use Today and the period calendar, Little Us stores your reported mood, desire, energy and comfort, their dates and timestamps, period starts and optional ends, approximate or missing-start annotations, a usual interval if you provide one, and timing estimates with their recorded basis. These are sensitive personal and health records. They are used to show your reports, calendar and rough date estimates. Calendar dates never populate mood, desire, energy or comfort.

Record identifiers, revisions, operation receipts and timestamps support syncing, corrections, Undo and recovery. Pending changes and confirmed records are stored in the app's local database. Confirmed shared records are stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the United States. An offline change stays on the phone until it can sync.

Who can access the records

Both authenticated household members can read all check-ins, period records and estimates automatically. Each person can change their own records. The shared activity catalog can be managed by both people. A compact screen for one person does not limit that account's access to household history.

Google processes account and database information to provide Firebase Authentication and Firestore. Alex, as the trusted Firebase administrator, can access cloud records and create household recovery archives. The app uses authenticated household access controls and encrypted network connections. Records are readable by the authorized household accounts and administrator; they are not end-to-end encrypted between the two phones.

Little Us does not sell personal data, serve ads, use advertising trackers, or send health records to AI services. It has no Health Connect or Stardust integration. The app does not request location, contacts, camera or body-sensor access. Google services process operational information under Google's privacy policy.

Storage, exports and recovery

Records remain while the household uses the app unless they are cleared, corrected or removed through coordinated administrator cleanup. There is no automatic expiry of household history. Signing out or uninstalling does not delete confirmed cloud records. Signing in again can recover confirmed records. Signing out leaves pending saved actions on the original phone under the original account. Uninstalling or losing the phone can lose unsynced work.

Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for the app's databases and preferences. A pending-actions export can contain sensitive values in an unencrypted JSON file at a destination you choose. Keep the file private. Import it using the original account, and delete it when no longer needed. The destination provider's own storage and privacy rules apply.

Administrator recovery archives can include both members' confirmed records, operation receipts and history. Archive files remain until the administrator deletes them. Clearing a field removes its current value. Record removal can retain revision markers and operation history to prevent stale retries. These actions do not erase every device cache or previously exported file. Restoring an older archive can bring back older records.

Correction and deletion requests

You can correct or clear your own check-ins and period records in the app. Contact Alex at aleponce92@gmail.com to request account or household-data deletion, including removal from cloud storage and administrator-held archives. This requires coordinated administrator cleanup; the app has no automatic account-deletion button. The administrator will explain the affected shared records and any retained operation history before carrying out the request. You control copies you exported or saved with another provider.

Period estimates and medical information

Period estimates are rough timing guides based on recorded starts and an optional initial usual interval. They do not confirm ovulation or fertility and must not be used for contraception. Missing or approximate records can limit the available estimate.

Little Us is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
